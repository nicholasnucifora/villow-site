## Final prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create ONE finished transparent-background website illustration for Villow's 'One experience. Across your devices.' section. Reference image is STYLE ONLY: borrow its soft rounded cartoon forms, restrained sage/mint, deep teal and warm cream palette, simple gentle gradients and friendly quiet character. Do not reproduce its viewer, projector, scene or edge artifacts. New composition: a small grounded still life of an open laptop, a tablet propped beside it and a phone in front, all physically believable and separately recognizable. Across the three screens, one simple mint rolling-hill landscape with a small pale sun repeats, suggesting a shared experience. No app UI or text. Draw these as charming illustrated objects with softly dimensional cream bodies and muted green edges, not thin outlined icons, wireframes, generic tech logos or photorealistic hardware. Minimal detail, very few colours, large clean shapes, light soft shading only; no texture, gloss, complicated keyboards, tiny decorations, lines linking devices, arrows or labels. All devices rest together on one low soft pale sage ground shape; no floating in the sky. Wide composition with generous transparent space around the group, nothing cropped. Match the warmth and simplicity of the reference while leaving the website typography to HTML. Transparent alpha background; no backdrop, no checkerboard, no watermark, no text.
