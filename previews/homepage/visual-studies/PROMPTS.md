# F — visual direction studies

These are static image mockups made with the built-in image generation tool. They do not replace C or change the live site. All three use the same working copy and action. Product controls in F3 are illustrative, not literal screenshots.

Grounding: `src/content/pages/features.yaml` and `src/content/pages/extension.yaml`. Channel filtering and cadence, saving discoveries for later, and optional reflection are verified capabilities. No automatic setting changes or universal browser coverage are implied.

## F1 — A world with room

```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset: a single, finished desktop homepage opening visual study for Villow. Flat front-on web design screenshot, approximately 1600 by 1000, no browser chrome, no presentation frame, no device mockup. The composition must be memorable and art-directed, with generous readable typography and a clear action. This is a visual identity exploration, not a literal shipping UI.
Brand: Villow is a calm, personal app for watching YouTube with user-chosen controls. It has a rounded friendly wordmark and an evergreen/mint identity with a subtle connection to nature. Its deeper story is shaping subscriptions, discovery and habits around the viewer's own choices. A finite feed is one of several capabilities. This is not an anti-YouTube comparison or a wellbeing clinic.
Render this exact shared copy, all clearly legible:
Small wordmark in header: "Villow"
Main headline: "YouTube, shaped by you."
Body: "Villow is an app for watching YouTube with a finite feed and controls you choose. Shape a channel’s uploads, save discoveries for later, and reflect on what felt worthwhile."
Single prominent action: "Get started"
Small header link: "How it works"
Only these marketing words. Do not add a slogan, claims, availability badges, testimonials, metrics, download counts, star ratings, invented features, extra CTAs, or tiny paragraphs. The brand and body must be readable. No comparison, no before/after grid, no thumbnail videos, no full dashboard screenshot, no three-video stopping metaphor. Do not let artwork run behind small body text. The entire opening is visible.
Art direction: a contemporary illustrated world, quiet but rich in feeling. Treat this as beautiful visual storytelling with real softness, depth and lighting, not clip-art mountains. An airy pearl-blue sky occupies most of the page; sculptural rolling mint and sea-glass hills and a few elegantly shaped willow/pine trees form a low landscape around the bottom and edges. The scene has subtle atmospheric depth, softly rounded forms, very restrained painterly texture and warm early sun. Detail is selective and purposeful; a still, open, inviting place rather than an elaborate fantasy painting. The headline is very large rounded dark-ink lettering with confident, slightly playful proportions, integrated into the open sky; the body and evergreen action sit in a clean quiet area. Let a beautiful curved tree canopy or near hill give the composition dimension and an unexpected crop while leaving every word unobstructed. Careful horizon, no triangular SVG forests, no individual blades of grass, no cottagecore clutter, no stock watercolor look, no 3D glossy plastic. The image should have the considered simplicity of a well-loved illustrated book cover, adapted to an exceptionally clear homepage. No UI fragments in this version. The feeling is room to choose, not an illustration that tries to explain the entire product. Make the landscape beautifully restrained and the typography decisive.
```
