Getting started

Set up Villow.

You can change your choice here at any time.

Set up or join a Villow.

Use your own installation, or join someone you trust.

Create your own Villow.

Villow Setup guides you through creating your private Villow. Use it yourself, or invite friends. Setup takes about 30 minutes.

Hosting for friends may eventually require paid plans. See hosting costs and sharing

macOS and Linux support coming soon.

Set up manually

Join a friend’s Villow.

Joining a friend’s Villow is simple. If you create your own, you can invite friends to join this way too.

  1. Ask them to invite your Google account.
  2. Open their Villow link and sign in.

Choose a host you trust

The owner can access data stored in their Villow, such as your viewing history and usage. Signing in with Google doesn’t give them your Google password.

Understand permissions and privacy
The extension comes next.Once your Villow is ready, it will guide you through installing and connecting the extension. Preview setup now.

Where will you use the extension?

Add Villow to your browser.

Available for
  • Chrome
  • Firefox
  • Edge

Once installed, finish connecting it in your Villow.

Use Firefox on Android

  1. Install Firefox if you don’t have it.
    Get Firefox
  2. Open the add-on listing in Firefox, then add Villow.
  3. Open your Villow in Firefox to connect it.

Choose Firefox if your device asks which app to use.

Use YouTube in the browser where you installed Villow. The extension doesn’t run in the YouTube app.

Use Orion on iPhone or iPad

  1. Install Orion.
    Get Orion
  2. In Orion, open Settings → Extensions and enable Chrome extensions.
  3. Open this listing in Orion, then add Villow.
  4. Open your Villow in Orion to connect it.

Safari support isn’t available yet.

Use YouTube in the browser where you installed Villow. The extension doesn’t run in the YouTube app.

Connect it to Villow

In Villow, open Extension and select Create connect link. Paste the link into the extension’s settings, select Connect, then open YouTube in that browser.

Where will you use the extension?

Add Villow to your browser.

Available for
  • Chrome
  • Firefox
  • Edge

Once installed, open YouTube and choose your limits in the extension’s settings.

Use Firefox on Android

  1. Start with Firefox.

    Install Firefox on your phone or tablet if you don’t have it.

    Get Firefox
  2. Add Villow.

    Open this listing in Firefox and follow the installation prompts.

  3. Watch in Firefox.

    Open YouTube in Firefox, then choose your limits in Villow’s extension settings.

Choose Firefox if your device asks which app to use.

Use YouTube in the browser where you installed Villow. The extension doesn’t run in the YouTube app.

  1. Start with Orion.

    Install Orion on your iPhone or iPad.

    Get Orion
  2. Enable Chrome extensions.

  3. Add Villow.

  4. Watch in Orion.

    Open YouTube in Orion, then choose your limits in Villow’s extension settings.
