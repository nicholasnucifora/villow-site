Preset 30 derives from saved 29 with independent CSS and its own repaired
artwork. The earlier presets and their artwork remain available unchanged
apart from shared preview navigation.

LANDSCAPE
- Built-in image_gen cleaned the foreground hill's cutout fringe and stray
  pixels while retaining the low contour and sage shading.
- The tree cutout has extended hidden bases. A second targeted image edit
  removed the artificial curved join where the old cutout ended. This lets
  the tree groups sit a little higher and reveals the trailing rounded
  shrubs without exposing gaps below them. The bases stay behind the hill.
- CSS retains 29's wide-screen landscape height and tree sizes. The left
  group rises by 14–32px, the right by 8–16px, with dedicated phone offsets.
- At <=700px the whole landscape rises 44px by reducing lower hero spacing
  from 184px to 140px. The CTA stays, the cinema stays hidden, and all of
  28/29's centred stacked marketing sections are retained.

FOOTER
Retains W's existing p-meadow-simple.svg shapes and colours. Its image
height is now independent of text height and bounded between 160–510px;
the visible band is 72–130px. A 900px minimum artwork width on small screens
shows broad, shallow bends instead of squeezing both waves onto the phone.
This also removes the unnecessarily deep blank footer scenery area.
