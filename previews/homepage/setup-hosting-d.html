Hosting your Villow.

You can start hosting for free.

Villow is free, and you can start with free hosting plans. Sharing with friends is where it gets even better—we highly recommend it.

For a shared Villow, plan for the cost of Vercel, and possibly Supabase too as your group’s usage grows. You can stay on eligible free plans until you reach their limits. Here’s what to look out for.

Your first stop: Admin

Check your group’s usage, manage members and see System health. Admin has the context of your own Villow, so start there for the details that apply to you.

Open your Villow →and choose Admin

Each service has its own allowance. If something stops working, start here.

A little help with hosting

Choose a question. The answer opens right here.

YouTube API

Why aren’t new videos appearing?

Open Home again first—that’s when Villow checks for new subscription videos.

If nothing changes, check your allowance in Google Cloud ↗: choose your project, then YouTube Data API v3 → Quotas & System Limits. If it’s used up, wait for the daily reset. When does it reset? ↓

If there’s still allowance left, get help with the error ↓.

When does the daily allowance reset?

YouTube’s daily allowance resets at midnight Pacific Time. Everyone on your Villow shares it, so a busy day can use it up sooner.

Removing a member won’t undo usage already counted. See YouTube’s allowance guide ↗.

Does paying for hosting give us more YouTube allowance?

No. YouTube’s allowance is separate from Vercel and Supabase. Upgrading either hosting plan won’t increase it.

Start by checking Admin → App API usage to understand what your group is using. For a larger allowance, Google has its own request and review process ↗.

Vercel

My Villow won’t open—or says it’s paused

Open your Vercel dashboard ↗ and check the notice on your project or team. Vercel will tell you whether it’s a usage limit or another problem.

If it’s a Hobby usage limit, you can ask for a one-time extension ↓ before deciding whether to wait or upgrade. Admin will be available again once your Villow is back online.

If a deployment failed instead, copy its error message and ask for help ↓.

Can I get another month before paying for Vercel?

You can ask for a one-time courtesy boost. This can get your Villow running again and give you more time to see how your group’s usage goes before deciding to pay.

  1. Open Vercel support ↗ and sign in with the account you used to set up Villow.
  2. Choose Vercel, then select the team containing your Villow project.
  3. If the notice confirms a Hobby usage-limit pause, ask for a boost. You can use this message:
    My Villow has been paused after reaching a Hobby usage limit. Could I get a one-time courtesy boost so I can review our usage before deciding whether to upgrade?
  4. If offered, complete the request form and wait for confirmation. Check the expiry, reopen Villow, then use Admin to review your group’s usage.

One Villow host received 3× the allowance for 30 days. Vercel decides the offer and duration, so an extra month isn’t guaranteed. See Vercel’s boost guidance ↗.

When should I pay for Vercel?

For a Villow shared regularly with friends, budget for a paid plan as usage grows. You can begin on Hobby if your use fits its personal, non-commercial terms ↗ and allowances.

Use the notices and usage information in your dashboard to decide when to upgrade. Vercel Pro starts at US$20/month ↗; extra usage, seats and taxes can add to that.

Will reaching a free limit charge me automatically?

Reaching a free allowance doesn’t automatically upgrade you to a paid plan. A service may pause or restrict what your Villow can do instead.

If you choose a paid plan, Vercel and Supabase bill you directly. Review Vercel’s spending controls ↗ and Supabase’s cost controls ↗ first—some extra charges aren’t covered by a cap.

Supabase

Supabase is paused, or things aren’t saving

Open your Supabase dashboard ↗ and read the project’s notice.

Paused after inactivity? Free projects can pause after a week without activity. Follow the restore prompt; this alone doesn’t mean you need to pay.

Reached an allowance? The notice will explain what’s affected. Check whether it’s time to upgrade ↓.

When might we need a paid Supabase plan?

You may need to upgrade when your group outgrows the free space or data allowances. There isn’t a set number of friends that triggers this.

Supabase Pro starts at US$25/month ↗. Extra projects, usage and taxes may cost more. Check your dashboard’s usage and the current plan before choosing.

Is my group’s data backed up?

Don’t assume it is. Check the backup options for your plan ↗. On a free plan, keep regular exports outside the project. Database backups don’t include uploaded files in Supabase Storage.

As host, you can access the group’s stored data. Let friends know, and keep your hosting accounts secure.

Our full restore walkthrough is still being checked. Ask for help ↗ before relying on a backup or deleting anything.

Your Villow

How do I invite friends—and why can’t they join?

Open Admin → Invite friends and follow the steps there. If your Google project is in Testing, add each friend’s Google email under Google Auth → Audience → Test users ↗.

Send the invite link from Admin and ask them to use that same Google account. For a returning friend, check their access under Admin → People.

Why can’t I sign in?

Use the Google account you were invited with. If Google shows an access error, ask the host to check the invitation steps ↓.

If the app opens but sign-in still fails, check whether Supabase is paused ↓. The host can also check your access in Admin → People.

Still stuck? Get help with the exact error ↓.

How do I update my Villow?

A new Villow release doesn’t necessarily update your hosted app automatically. Follow the instructions for your version and make a backup first.

Our owner update walkthrough is still being checked. Ask on Discord ↗ before redeploying or changing your database.

What if I want to stop hosting?

Tell your friends first—they’ll lose access when your Villow goes offline. Uninstalling the setup app doesn’t close your hosted projects or cancel subscriptions.

Review your Vercel, Supabase and Google Cloud projects and billing. Deleting Supabase permanently removes this Villow’s data and its provider-held backups; it doesn’t delete anyone’s Google account.

Ask for help planning the shutdown ↗ before deleting projects. The full walkthrough is still being checked.

Something else isn’t working. Where can I get help?

Check Vercel status ↗ and Supabase status ↗ for an outage first.

Then ask our Discord community ↗. Include what happened, whether it affects everyone and the error message or a screenshot. Leave out passwords, API keys and other private information.

We’re here to help.

You don’t have to work it all out alone.
