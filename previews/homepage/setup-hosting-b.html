Hosting your Villow.

You can share your Villow with friends. As your group grows, you may reach the limits of the services that run it.

Vercel and Supabase offer paid plans if you need more room; you pay them directly, and Villow receives none of that money. Your group also shares a separate daily YouTube allowance.

Your group

Everyone shares a daily YouTube allowance: normally 10,000 units for most requests and 100 search calls. It resets at midnight Pacific Time.

Paying for hosting doesn’t increase it.

Manage your group ↓

Vercel

Vercel hosts your Villow. If you reach a free-plan limit, it may pause your site.

Pro starts at US$20/month if you choose to upgrade.

Vercel limits & recovery ↓

Supabase

Supabase stores your data and handles sign-in. It has separate free-plan limits.

Pro starts at US$25/month if you choose to upgrade.

Supabase limits & recovery ↓

Prices are in US dollars; taxes and additional usage may apply.

When something goes wrong.

Here’s where to look and what you can do.

Your group & YouTube

Everyone on your Villow shares a daily YouTube allowance. If it runs out, feed updates and searches can stop until it resets; videos already loaded may still play.

In Admin → App API usage, see which people or activities are using it. To remove someone, choose Revoke access in Admin → People; you can restore their access later.

More about the daily allowance

The default is 10,000 units a day for most YouTube data requests, plus 100 search calls. Allowances reset at midnight Pacific Time; check your own project in the Google Cloud API dashboard ↗ under YouTube Data API v3 → Quotas & System Limits. See YouTube’s quota rules ↗.

Revoking access prevents future use; it doesn’t reset usage already counted or delete that person’s data. Paying Vercel or Supabase doesn’t increase your YouTube allowance. Google does allow quota extension requests ↗, subject to an audit and approval.

Vercel: your Villow is paused

If you see this screen, check the email and Usage page for your team in the Vercel dashboard ↗ to find out why.

If hosting is paused, everyone is affected and Admin won’t be available until it’s restored.

Recover from a Hobby usage limit

Contact Vercel support ↗ and ask about a one-time courtesy boost. If offered, it can temporarily restore your projects while you review usage; Vercel determines eligibility and terms. Read Vercel’s boost guidance ↗.

Once Villow is back, review your group and usage. You can also follow the timing in Vercel’s notice or consider upgrading; don’t assume a pause will automatically end at the start of a month. See Vercel’s Hobby limits ↗.

Supabase: data or sign-in isn’t working

Check your project notices and your organisation’s Usage in the Supabase dashboard ↗. A generic error doesn’t necessarily mean you’ve hit a limit.

Check a Supabase limit

Look for the affected allowance, such as database size, storage or data transfer, and follow the notice for that specific limit. Reducing future activity won’t erase usage already counted.

If your database is read-only or its disk is full, follow Supabase’s database recovery guidance ↗. Removing people won’t shrink data already stored.

Considering a paid plan?

You can start with the free allowances and decide later. If you reach a limit, you may need to upgrade the affected service—not necessarily both.

Vercel

Pro starts at US$20/month. In your Vercel dashboard ↗, select your team and go to Settings → Billing → Plan to review an upgrade. See Vercel pricing ↗.

Supabase

Pro starts at US$25/month. In your Supabase dashboard ↗, select your organisation and go to Billing → Subscription Plan to review an upgrade. See Supabase pricing ↗.

Prices are in US dollars. Taxes, extra projects or usage can add to the cost. Check the current plan and its limits before paying.

Before upgrading

On Vercel, a team owner or billing role may be needed. Review Spend Management ↗ and any usage charges.

Supabase plans apply to the organisation. Review its projects and cost controls ↗; the spend cap doesn’t cover every possible charge.
