Hosting your Villow.

Villow is free to use. When you host a Villow for friends, it runs on services you control. Here’s how to manage your group, understand costs and get help.

How do these services work together?

Your friends open the app hosted on Vercel. It gets video information from YouTube and saves your group’s accounts and activity in Supabase. Google handles Google sign-in; Supabase stores Villow’s account data.

You look after these service accounts. Everyday group management happens inside Villow; hosting usage and billing live in the provider dashboards.

What does hosting cost?

Start for free. Upgrade only if you need to. Eligible personal, non-commercial groups can use the free plans, within each provider’s allowances.

Prices in US dollars; taxes and extra usage may apply. Extra projects or seats can add to the bill. Vercel Hobby ↗ is for personal, non-commercial use.

Will I be charged automatically?

Villow doesn’t charge for hosting. If you choose paid services, Vercel and Supabase bill you directly, including applicable usage charges. Check their billing settings and spending controls before upgrading.

What happens when I reach a free limit?
  • YouTube: data requests can stop until the daily quota resets. Paying for hosting doesn’t increase it. Check video updates.
  • Vercel: a Hobby usage limit can pause hosting. Follow the timing and recovery options in your provider notice. Get your Villow back online.
  • Supabase: the response depends on the allowance. Separately, free projects can pause after a week of inactivity. Check your project’s status.

You may only need to upgrade one service. Before paying, review Vercel spending controls ↗ and Supabase cost controls ↗; not every charge is covered by a spend cap.

Something not working?

Start with what you’re experiencing. These checks work even when you can’t get to Admin.

My Villow won’t open

What’s happening?

Your Villow won’t load, shows a deployment error or says it’s temporarily paused.

How to check

Open your project and team Usage in the Vercel dashboard ↗. Check provider emails for a specific error or limit.

How to fix it

  1. Try the project’s Vercel address. If that opens but your custom domain doesn’t, check the project’s Domains settings.
  2. If the deployment failed, open its build logs and start with the reported error. Ask for help if you’re unsure what it means.
  3. If Hobby hosting is paused, follow Vercel’s notice. You can ask Vercel support ↗ about a one-time courtesy boost; eligibility and terms are up to Vercel. See its pause guidance ↗.
  4. Once restored, review usage before deciding whether to wait, reduce future activity or upgrade. Admin is unavailable while hosting is paused.

Still having problems? Ask on Discord ↗.

I can’t sign in

What’s happening?

The app opens, but sign-in fails or you can’t access your account.

How to check

Check project status and notices in the Supabase dashboard ↗. If Google shows the error, also check your project’s Google Auth settings ↗.

How to fix it

  1. If Supabase says the project is paused, follow its restore instructions. An inactivity pause doesn’t automatically mean you need a paid plan.
  2. If you see a usage or database notice, follow the steps for that specific allowance. Don’t assume every sign-in error is a quota issue.
  3. If Google rejects sign-in, confirm you’re using the invited Google account. Check its Testing audience and that the configured redirect address matches the one required by your installation.
  4. Try signing in again after resolving the reported issue. If only one person is affected, the host can also check their access in Admin → People.

Still having problems? Ask on Discord ↗.

Videos aren’t updating

What’s happening?

New videos or searches aren’t appearing. A shared YouTube allowance or a failed request could be the cause; already loaded videos may still play.

How to check

In the Google Cloud API dashboard ↗, select your project, then YouTube Data API v3 → Quotas & System Limits. Admin → App API usage can help explain activity, but Google’s dashboard is authoritative.

How to fix it

  1. Open Home again: Villow fetches subscription videos when each person opens Home.
  2. If the quota is exhausted, wait until midnight Pacific Time, when daily YouTube quotas reset ↗. Review group usage before then.
  3. If quota remains, look for failed requests in the Vercel project’s runtime logs. Note the error and time for support.
  4. If recommendations or older video details are stale, also check the daily /api/sync job in Vercel’s Cron Jobs settings. That job handles maintenance, not fetching new subscription videos.

Paying Vercel or Supabase doesn’t increase YouTube quota. Revoking a member’s access won’t reset usage already counted.

Still having problems? Ask on Discord ↗.

My friends can’t join

What’s happening?

A friend has your link but can’t get into your Villow.

How to check

Open Admin → Invite friends and check which Google account your friend is using. If you can’t open Admin, use the provider checks above.

How to fix it

  1. If your Google project is in Testing, open Google Auth platform → Audience ↗ and add their Google email under Test users.
  2. Share the Villow link from Admin → Invite friends. Ask them to use the Google email associated with the YouTube account they want to sign in with.
  3. For someone who has joined before, check Admin → People. Restore their access if you intended to let them return.

Still having problems? Ask on Discord ↗.

Something else isn’t working

What’s happening?

You’re seeing another error, or the checks above haven’t explained the problem.

How to check

Check Vercel status ↗ and Supabase status ↗ for an outage, then note any error shown in Villow.

How to fix it

  1. Reload once and note whether the problem affects you, one friend or everyone.
  2. Write down what happened, when it happened and what you’ve already tried. Include the exact error message or a screenshot with private information removed.
  3. Ask the community for help. Never share passwords, API keys, access tokens or unredacted logs.

Still having problems? Ask on Discord ↗.

Common questions

How do I update my Villow?

Keep track of Villow releases and follow the instructions for your installation. Don’t assume a new release updates your hosted app automatically: changes can involve both the app and its database.

Before updating, check the release instructions and make a recoverable backup. The supported owner update walkthrough is still being verified. Ask on Discord ↗ for guidance for your version before redeploying or changing the database.

How do I keep my group’s data safe?

As the host, you control the Supabase project and can access the data stored there. Let friends know this before they join, protect your hosting accounts and keep a recovery plan.

Check Supabase’s backup guidance ↗ for your plan. Free projects need regular exports kept outside the project. Database backups don’t include files in Supabase Storage. A Villow-specific restore walkthrough is still being verified; ask for help before relying on a backup.

What happens if I stop hosting?

You can stop, but let your friends know first: they’ll lose access to this Villow when it goes offline. Uninstalling the setup app doesn’t close your hosted projects or cancel provider subscriptions.

Review your Vercel, Supabase and Google Cloud projects, connections and billing. Agree what to keep or delete before closing anything. Deleting the Supabase project permanently removes its data and provider-held backups, including this Villow’s account records; it doesn’t delete anyone’s Google account.

The full shutdown walkthrough is still being verified. Ask for help planning a shutdown ↗ before deleting projects.
