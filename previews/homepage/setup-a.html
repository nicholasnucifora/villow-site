Getting started

Find your way into Villow.

Choose the web app for a finite feed without YouTube’s personalised recommendations. Use the extension if you mainly watch on YouTube. Connect both for YouTube discovery alongside Villow’s insights and friends.

Three ways to use Villow

FeatureVillow web appFor a different feedExtension on its ownFor watching on YouTubeWeb app + extensionFor moving between both
The experienceVillow web app

A finite feed from subscriptions and videos popular in your country. YouTube’s personalised recommendations aren’t included.

Extension on its own

Watch on YouTube, including its personalised recommendations, with limits you choose.

Web app + extension

Use Villow’s feed and discover on YouTube with the extension. Save videos from YouTube into Villow.

Your habitsVillow web app

Optional ratings, viewing insights and gentle nudges help you understand and shape your watching.

Extension on its own

See today’s usage in this browser. Villow’s insights and suggestions aren’t included.

Web app + extension

Your connected YouTube usage also informs Villow’s insights and gentle nudges.

FriendsVillow web app

Watch together, share playlists and exchange video recommendations with friends.

Extension on its own

No social features.

Web app + extension

The web app’s shared watching, playlists and friends’ recommendations.

Across devicesVillow web app

Your feed, progress, viewing time and reflections stay together across phone, tablet and computer.

Extension on its own

Separate limits and usage in each browser where you install it.

Web app + extension

Usage and supported limits stay together across Villow and every browser you connect.

Villow web app

Your own place to watch.

Create a private Villow, or join one run by someone you trust. Each person chooses their own viewing settings.

Allow about 30 minutes

Create your own Villow

Villow Setup guides you through creating your private installation. Once it’s ready, you can invite friends.

Set up Villow

Use a Windows computer for Setup. You’ll need Google, Google Cloud, Vercel and Supabase accounts.

Download link being prepared.

Setup for macOS and Linux is still undecided. Villow itself opens in a browser on phone, tablet and computer.

Prefer manual setup?

No hosting setup

Join a friend’s Villow

Open your friend’s link, sign in and choose your viewing settings. The owner needs to allow your Google account to sign in.

Choose someone you trust. They control the hosting and database and can access the data stored there.

Understand permissions and privacy

Free software.
Hosting is your choice.

Hosting uses Vercel and Supabase, whose free allowances may not cover a group.

Hosting, costs and sharing

If both Pro plans are needed, current base prices start at US$45/month combined, before tax and extra usage. Your total depends on plans and usage.

Vercel pricingSupabase pricing

Try a small group and monitor usage. You can upgrade, keep your group smaller, or revoke access later. Revoking access does not reset quotas or remove existing charges.

You decide what hosting yourself and friends is worth to you.

Running your Villow

Web app + extension

Connect them when you’re ready.

Create or join a Villow, then install the extension. Open a connect link from Villow in each browser you want to include.

Your connected YouTube time joins your Villow usage, with supported limits shared between them.

Villow extension

Start in your browser.

Choose daily time, video and recommendation limits, with calmer settings for YouTube. Install it in the browser you want to use.

ChromeChrome Web Store EdgeEdge Add-ons FirefoxFirefox Add-ons

The extension works in supported browsers, not native YouTube apps or TVs. Connect each browser to include its YouTube usage in Villow.
