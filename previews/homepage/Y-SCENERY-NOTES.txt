Art direction
- Retains W's original wide landscape above 1600px, including its soft gradients,
  sage palette, pale sunrise, clouds and trees.
- Two image_gen edits recompose that same supplied image for tablet and phone.
  These remain AI-generated illustrations, not human retouching or layered art.
- Phone: assets/y-landscape/phone-source.png, 1536 x 1024.
- Tablet: assets/y-landscape/tablet-source.png, 1774 x 887.
- Matching WebP delivery files are 15 KB and 21 KB. Encoding only; no stretching.
- Exact generation prompts/reference/mode: assets/y-landscape/provenance.json.
- Sources select at 700px and 1600px. CSS crops the scenery edges around the sun;
  peripheral trees/clouds can leave the frame. No per-device JS.

Layout
- Refinement after the user's screenshots: the landscape fills the hero behind
  its text and cinema, rather than appearing as a smaller strip underneath.
- Header, body and hero share #fdfdfd, sampled from the image's neutral sky.
  The old blue-white page background is removed from Y's opening scene.
- Landscape scale is anchored to a minimum art height (900px phone, 1000px
  tablet, 740px wide). Width changes crop the sides rather than miniaturising
  the hills and sun. Larger screens can expand the original wide image to fill.
- A phone-only sky fade keeps birds away from the stacked copy; sun and hills
  stay behind the cinema. Images retain their natural proportions.
- The cinema uses its natural 2:1 stage at every size. Content sets the stacked
  hero's height; the two-column hero has room for text above the horizon.
- Copy and cinema remain beside each other above 700px, stacking below.
- Narrow artwork is capped at 520px. Small phones gain 20px around the scene;
  the reflection prompt also fits inside the original player at 320px.
- The footer reuses W's existing SVG curves at their original aspect ratio in
  an independent decorative band. Closing text no longer stretches the hills.
- render-y.mjs derives from saved W without replacing its cinema or interactions.
  All changes are in this preview folder. X stays retired. Nothing published.
