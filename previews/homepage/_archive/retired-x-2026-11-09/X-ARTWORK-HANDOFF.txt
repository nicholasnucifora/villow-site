A PRACTICAL ROUTE TO FINISHED ART

1. Approve the direction with one scene.
   Decide whether this simpler cinema composition is the right direction.
   Keep Villow's warm, calm palette, rounded shapes and playful cat.
   A small style sample plus one finished hero is enough to approve a look
   before commissioning the remaining illustrations.

2. Ask an illustrator for an original redraw.
   If human-made final art is the requirement, state that explicitly in the
   brief: no generated assets, original drawing, editable source files,
   agreed website usage rights and agreed credit. Treat X and W as visual
   direction, not artwork that only needs tracing or surface cleanup.
   If doing it yourself, draw the same simple shapes in a vector editor and
   follow the layer/size requirements below.

3. Deliver assets that are easy to integrate.
   Supply an editable source and optimized SVG exports. Keep person/chair,
   projector, screen frame, cat and ground as separate named groups.
   Keep screen geometry and the person's position identical between the
   watching and thinking poses. Avoid small hand/finger details that become
   ambiguous on phones. The play button, progress slider and rating prompt
   stay real HTML controls, not text painted into the illustration.

4. Approve desktop and phone together.
   Check the complete composition and individual silhouettes at actual
   display size. Use a compact composition or stack below the text when the
   art would otherwise become too small. Preserve proportions of people,
   faces and screens; crop scenery only. Test at 320, 390, 768, 861, 1024,
   1280 and 1920px, plus 200% zoom and a real phone before publication.
   Check play/pause, seeking, optional rating and reduced motion.

5. Expand the approved style into a small asset set.
   Suggested order: hero, the five watching-story characters, then any
   remaining generated thumbnails or streak artwork that should be replaced.
   Review every displayed image if the final site must contain no generated
   artwork. Keep an asset register with source, creator and usage permission.
   Product diagrams and simple section curves can remain editable code.

ART DIRECTION
