Shared copy and product grounding

YouTube, shaped by you.

Villow is an app for watching YouTube with a finite feed and controls you choose. Shape a channel’s uploads, save discoveries for later, and reflect on what felt worthwhile.

Get started

These studies explore the opening’s visual character. Villow supports channel filters and delivery schedules, saving discoveries for later, and optional reflection. A connected extension can send discovered videos to Villow before playback. Reflection may inform explained suggestions; the viewer chooses any adjustments.

The artwork and lettering were generated for this exploration. The existing brand assets and live site remain in place.
