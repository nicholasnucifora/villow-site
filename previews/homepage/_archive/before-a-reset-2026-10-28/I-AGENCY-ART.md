Beside them near the lower centre-left is a tiny recognisable film projector casting ONE soft, bounded cone of pale warm light to the RIGHT. At the right edge of that light is a single frameless illustrated video scene: a simple amusing terracotta cat curled up in an oversized mint-green chair. This projected image should read as enjoyable leisure, not education or productivity. The person is visibly in control of the projector. Person and projector occupy the left 40%; the short light cone and projected scene occupy the right 60%; a clear gap of transparent sky around the silhouette. No border or white card around the scene. Wide landscape composition approximately 4:3, compact overall silhouette, generous transparent padding all around, nothing clipped. Style: unusually clean, confident contemporary flat cartoon illustration, broad simple silhouettes, a few very restrained smooth gradients for softness; match a calm minimalist landscape made of rounded sage hills. Very limited palette: forest teal #285e53, sage green #96bfa7, pale mint #daecdf, muted warm cream #f3dfb2, tiny warm peach skin/cat accent. Minimal human facial detail, relaxed natural posture, accurate simple anatomy, no visible detailed fingers necessary. Clear scene hierarchy and visual warmth, no busy textures. No extra furniture, plants, shrubs, trees, hills, sun, landscape, ground strip, floating icons, badges, speech bubbles, confetti, diagrams or UI. No text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, metrics or controls painted onto the image. No paper collage, hard rectangular player, photorealism, 3D render, glossy plastic, grain, watercolor, painterly detail or decorative effects. Keep it sleek and spare, but recognisable as someone choosing how to watch. Real alpha transparency, including the open space around the scene; use light cone translucency.
