PRESET Z — LAYERED LANDSCAPE
10 November 2026
Preview: http://127.0.0.1:4411/z.html

SCOPE
The user explicitly requested a new preset for this work. Y's main content
and y-responsive-scenery.css remain byte-for-byte unchanged; only shared
preview navigation was updated to expose Z. W remains available too.
No production-site changes or publication are part of this work.

COMPOSITION
Z keeps the existing woman/projector/cat artwork and controls. The landscape
uses a wide background and a compact phone background, independent left and
right tree groups, and an independent transparent foreground hill. The sun
and its glow remain together in each background. Trees sit behind the nearest
hill and have their own responsive sizes and offsets.

The wide background scales proportionally to the page width. Below 701px the
content stacks and uses the compact composition. That background has a 480px
minimum width and a right-aligned crop on the narrowest phones; the separate
tree groups remain visible. Content-driven hero height and bottom spacing
keep the hills close while providing a gap between sun and cinema cloud.
Header and hero use #fdfdfd, with a fade at the top of the background image.

EDITING
render-z.mjs derives Z from the saved Y page without modifying Y.
z-layered-landscape.css contains all Z-only layout and layer positioning.
Change .z-scenery-trees-left/right width and bottom values to move the groups.
The foreground stays at its natural aspect ratio along the bottom edge.
Z copies Y's existing footer treatment in its own stylesheet.

ARTWORK
Mode: built-in image_gen edits / transparent extraction of supplied art.
This is still AI-generated artwork. It is not human retouching.
assets/z-layered/provenance.json records the exact final prompts, references
and generated source paths for all five assets. PNG source files and WebP
delivery files are saved beside it. The image tool removed the trees and
foreground from the backgrounds and reconstructed scenery behind them.
WebP encoding was the only post-generation asset processing.

VALIDATION
Browser measurements: 320, 390, 480, 600, 700, 701, 861, 1024, 1168, 1280,
1636, 1920 and 2560px. All scenery assets load, header/hero colours match,
and no horizontal overflow was found. Visual review includes phones,
the stacked/tablet boundary, intermediate desktop and wide desktop.
At 320px, keyboard seeking to the end reveals the rating prompt, Worth it
sets aria-pressed=true, and Home restores the beginning.
Existing page-navigation and semantic-position checks pass.

Responsive measurements: qa/z-responsive-checks.json.
