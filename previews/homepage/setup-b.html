Three ways to use Villow.

Villow app

  • A finite feed from subscriptions, videos popular in your country and friends’ recommendations.
  • Ratings, detailed viewing insights, suggested adjustments and settings to shape your experience.
  • Share videos and playlists, or watch together with friends.
  • Your feed, saved progress and viewing time stay in sync across devices.
Get Villow

Villow extension

  • YouTube as you know it, including its recommendations, with extra controls.
  • Set daily limits on time spent, recommendations seen and videos watched.
  • No Villow sharing or watch-together features.
  • No cross-device sync or detailed viewing insights. Usage stays in this browser.
Get the extension

Villow app + extension

  • Villow’s feed, plus videos you choose from YouTube’s recommendations using the extension.
  • App insights and suggested adjustments also include your connected YouTube usage.
  • Share videos and playlists, or watch together in the Villow app.
  • App progress syncs across devices; usage and supported limits sync across connected extensions.
Get the app and extension

Villow app

A good fit if you want to step away from YouTube’s personalised recommendations and endless Shorts scrolling.

Build a finite feed from subscriptions, videos popular in your country and friends’ recommendations. Customise what appears, choose your limits, and use detailed insights and suggested adjustments to shape your viewing.

Get Villow

Two ways to watch.

Villow extension

Quick start

Watch on YouTube with limits you choose. Try the extension first and add the Villow app later if you like.

A good fit if you’d rather keep watching on YouTube than switch to Villow to watch your chosen videos.

  • No Villow feed, friends or detailed insights.
  • Limits and usage stay separate in each browser.
Get the extension

Villow app + extension

Recommended

Choose a few videos from YouTube’s recommendations, then watch them in Villow. The connected extension adds your picks to your Villow feed.

We make switching easy, but that extra step is worth considering.

  • Villow’s feed, friends and detailed insights.
  • Usage and supported limits sync across connected extensions.
