My Villow won’t open

Your Villow won’t load, shows a deployment error or says it’s temporarily paused. Start with the service that keeps it online.

  1. Check the deployment

    Open your project in the Vercel dashboard ↗. If the latest deployment failed, open its build logs and note the reported error. Try the project’s Vercel address too; if that works but your custom domain doesn’t, check Domains in the project settings.

  2. Check for a usage limit

    Open your team’s Usage page and check emails from Vercel. A paused deployment affects everyone, including access to Admin. The provider notice explains which limit or issue needs attention.

  3. Follow the recovery steps

    Follow the timing and options in that notice. For a Hobby usage pause, you can ask Vercel support ↗ about a one-time courtesy boost; eligibility and terms are up to Vercel. See its pause guidance ↗. Once restored, review usage before deciding whether to wait, reduce future activity or upgrade.

Still having trouble? Ask on Discord ↗.

I can’t sign in

The app opens, but sign-in fails or you can’t access your account. The error message is the best place to start.

  1. Check your project’s status

    Open the Supabase dashboard ↗ and look for a pause, usage or database notice. If the project is paused after inactivity, follow its restore instructions. You don’t automatically need a paid plan.

  2. Check the Google account and sign-in settings

    If Google shows the error, confirm you’re using the invited Google account. In your project’s Google Auth settings ↗, check the Testing audience and the redirect address required by your installation.

  3. Retry after resolving the notice

    Follow the steps for the specific error or allowance, then sign in again. If only one person is affected, the host can also check whether their access was revoked in Admin → People.

Still having trouble? Ask on Discord ↗.

Videos aren’t updating

New videos or searches aren’t appearing. You may have reached the shared YouTube allowance, or a request may have failed. Already loaded videos may still play.

  1. Open Home again

    Villow fetches subscription videos when each person opens Home. Start there, and note any error you see.

  2. Check your YouTube allowance

    Open the Google Cloud API dashboard ↗, choose your project, then YouTube Data API v3 → Quotas & System Limits. If it’s exhausted, wait for the daily reset at midnight Pacific Time ↗. Admin → App API usage helps explain activity, but Google’s dashboard is authoritative.

  3. If quota remains, check for failed requests

    Open your Vercel project ↗ and check its runtime logs. Note the error and time for support. If recommendations or older video details are stale, also check the daily /api/sync job in Cron Jobs. That job handles maintenance, not new subscription videos.

Paid hosting doesn’t increase YouTube quota. Revoking a member’s access won’t reset usage already counted.

Still having trouble? Ask on Discord ↗.

My friends can’t join

A friend has your link but can’t get into your Villow. Check the invite steps and which Google account they’re using.

  1. Check your invitation settings

    Open Admin → Invite friends. If your Google project is in Testing, go to Google Auth platform → Audience ↗ and add your friend’s Google email under Test users.

  2. Send the right link

    Share the link from Admin → Invite friends. Ask your friend to use the Google email associated with the YouTube account they want to sign in with.

  3. Check returning members’ access

    If they joined before, look in Admin → People. Restore their access if you intended to let them return. If you can’t reach Admin, start with My Villow won’t open.

Still having trouble? Ask on Discord ↗.

Something else isn’t working

You’re seeing a different error, or the earlier checks haven’t explained the problem. A few details will help the community work it out.

  1. Check for an outage

    Look at Vercel status ↗ and Supabase status ↗. If there’s a relevant outage, follow the provider’s updates.

  2. Note what happens

    Reload once. Does the problem affect you, one friend or everyone? Write down what you did, when it happened and what you’ve tried. Include the exact error or a screenshot with private information removed.

  3. Ask the community

    Bring those details to Discord ↗. Never share passwords, API keys, access tokens or unredacted logs.

Still having trouble? Ask on Discord ↗.

Sharing an error or screenshot? Keep passwords, API keys and other private information out of it.
