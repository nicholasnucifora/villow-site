For creators Showcase

What if your video could come with a next step?

When a video is about a cause, a useful next step may happen off-screen. Villow's concept panel shows how a creator could attach one focused action beside a video. This is a showcase, not a live creator-submission feature.

How it could work

  • A creator attaches one focused next step to a video, such as a local group, an event, volunteering or a letter to a representative, with links they trust.
  • Viewers see it beside the video, instead of buried in the description.
  • Creators could see how many people took action, without identifying anyone.

A showcase, with more than one possible path

Native YouTube integration would need platform support, such as a creator control in YouTube Studio. An independent service could also accept verified creator submissions for Villow to display. Neither is available through this concept panel today.

Sometimes the best thing a video can do is get you off the couch.

Maybe, later
