Privacy policy

Website privacy policy

For the villow.app website. The browser extension and the web app have their own policies. All policies.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the website at villow.app, run by Fix the Web. It doesn't cover the browser extension or the Villow web app, which have their own policies.

Reading the site

The site has no analytics and no advertising. Its own code sets no cookies, and it loads nothing from other companies: its fonts, images and scripts all come from villow.app itself.

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives the technical details of each request, such as your IP address, your browser type and the page you asked for, so it can deliver the page and protect the site from attacks. If it needs to check a request for bots, it can set its own short-lived security cookie. Cloudflare handles all of this under its own privacy policy. We don't use it to track or profile visitors.

Supporting Villow

The support page is the one place the site collects anything, and only if you choose to contribute.

  • Payment. Stripe runs the checkout on its own page. Your card and billing details go to Stripe, never to us, and Stripe handles them under its own privacy policy.
  • What we keep. Our support service runs on Cloudflare. It stores the payment and subscription references Stripe sends us, the amount and date of each payment, whether your support is one-time or monthly and its status, your answer to the public-display question, and a protected fingerprint of your checkout email. The fingerprint is a keyed one-way code, not your address. It lets us match a later removal request to your contribution.
  • What can be public. Nothing, unless you answer "Yes" to the public-display question at checkout. Leaving it blank means no. With a Yes, your display name and message can appear on Villow and other Fix the Web projects, with the date and whether the support is one-time or monthly. Your amount is shown only if the question you answered asked about it. We review messages before they appear. We never publish your email address or payment details.
  • Removing your public details. Ask on the support page using your checkout email. We email you a confirmation link through Resend, our email provider. The link expires after 30 minutes. Confirming clears your name and message and stops them reappearing on later payments. It doesn't cancel monthly support. You do that in Stripe's customer portal, linked from the support page.
  • Stopping abuse. The support service limits how often one visitor can start a checkout or ask for removal. It counts requests for ten minutes against a keyed one-way code made from your IP address, not the address itself.
  • How long we keep it. Payment references are kept for our records and accounts. Removal links are cleared daily. Cloudflare's backups can hold older data for a short time.

Emailing us

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply to you.

Links to other sites

The site links to other services, including GitHub, Discord, Reddit, Stripe and the browser extension stores. Once you follow a link, that service's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. For questions or other data requests, contact us.
