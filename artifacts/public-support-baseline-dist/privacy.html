Privacy policies

Villow comes in three parts, and each one handles different information, so each has its own policy. Pick the one for what you're using. For a plain-language picture of how they fit together, read the privacy overview.

  • Browser extension

    What the extension reads on YouTube, what it keeps in your browser, and what it sends to the Villow you connect. This is the policy the Chrome, Edge and Firefox stores link to.

  • Web app · Draft

    Google sign-in and YouTube permissions, what a Villow stores, who can see it, and how to delete it.

  • This website

    What villow.app itself collects, which is very little, and what happens when you support Villow.
